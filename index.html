Luigi Marini

Lecturer in Politics, University College, Oxford

Luigi Marini, Oxford. Self-portrait. I am a political scientist and I work as a Lecturer in Politics at University College, Oxford, where I am also the Organising Tutor for Politics. I currently hold the same position at Merton College, and I regularly teach students and visiting students from across the University.

Previously, I was a DPhil student at Nuffield College and the Department of Politics and International Relations at the University of Oxford. I have an MSc from the Department of Goverment at the University of Essex and a Laurea (BA) from the Faculty of Political Sciences «Cesare Alfieri» at the University of Florence. I am originally from Sondrio, in Northern Italy.

My research primarily focuses on political representation and party competition. I study how social cleavages, economic inequalities and elite strategies shape politics in advanced industrial and post-industrial democracies, with a particular focus on Western Europe, and use of quantitative methods.

Fields of interest: Comparative Politics, Political Sociology, Political Economy, Political Behaviour, Economic History I am also interested more broadly in questions on electoral politics and voting behaviour, democracy and state formation.
